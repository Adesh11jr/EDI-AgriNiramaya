Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • First-Year Engineering EDI Project

1. Information We Collect

AgriNiramaya collects user account information (full name, email address, password hash, and security recovery answers), farm parcel descriptions (location, acreage, crop varieties), soil telemetry values (moisture, temperature, pH, humidity transmitted by field IoT nodes), and uploaded foliar photographs.

2. Security & Password Protection

In accordance with engineering standards, user passwords and security answers are never stored in plaintext. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 before insertion into the SQLite database. Session tokens are stored in secure session storage.

3. Telemetry & Leaf Image Storage

Foliar leaf images uploaded to AgriNiramaya are saved with sanitized UUID filenames in the local uploads/ directory. Images are associated exclusively with the authenticated user ID and are not shared with external third-party advertisers.

4. IoT Node Communication

ESP32 microcontrollers communicate with AgriNiramaya REST endpoints over local Wi-Fi or secure HTTP protocols. Microcontroller telemetry is restricted to physical agricultural parameters and does not transmit personal identifying device metadata.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

Users may modify or delete their farm parcels and associated records at any time directly through the Farm Management dashboard. For project evaluation inquiries, reach out through our Contact Page.
