Workflow

How AgriNiramaya Works

Four simple steps from field sensor deployment to actionable agronomic interventions.

01

Register Farm Parcels

Create farm profiles with location, acreage, specific crop varieties, and irrigation systems.

02

Deploy ESP32 Sensors

Connect capacitive soil moisture probes and DHT sensors to stream live field telemetry into the REST API.

03

Upload Foliar Images

Capture affected leaf photographs on your phone or camera to initiate diagnostic health assessment.

04

Receive Interventions

Review precision irrigation advice, lime or sulfur soil treatments, and targeted IPM care measures.

Impact

Measurable Benefits for Farmers

Early Pathogen Prevention

Maintain optimal root-zone pH (6.2 - 7.2) for peak bioavailability of macro and micronutrients.
