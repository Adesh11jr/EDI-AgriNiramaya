Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I test the system without an ESP32 hardware board?
A: Navigate to Soil Telemetry and click "Submit Test Telemetry". You can simulate moisture, temperature, and pH values which will be saved and classified cleanly.
Q: How are plant leaf images analyzed?
A: Images are securely stored in flask_project/uploads/. The backend CNN inference hook is structured to load model weights (.h5/.onnx) seamlessly.
